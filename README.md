# Hi, I'm Arka Dutta 👋

I'm a PhD student in Computing and Information Sciences at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Ashiqur R. KhudaBukhsh at the Social Insight Lab. My research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Computational Social Science, focusing on creating fair, responsible, and equitable Generative AI systems.

My work has appeared at ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, IJCAI, CoLM, and ICWSM, and has been covered by ABC News, CNN, WIRED, and the Montreal AI Ethics Institute.
