I'm a PhD student in Computing and Information Sciences at the Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Ashiqur R. KhudaBukhsh. I evaluate and post-train large language models for safety and trustworthiness. My evaluations find where models hallucinate under pressure, carry hidden bias, or infer sensitive attributes they were never given, and my current work turns those failures into post-training methods that make models safer.
